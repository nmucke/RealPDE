**FZ-EIKC + BVR + LOWKE + KD6 + KD7 + LSK** = fa948762 (KD7) + LSK (experiments/lsk/notes.md). LSK is a frozen separable
least-squares correction of the packed backbone output. It uses the top-8 SVD directions of the members' concatenated head
weight plus a constant, and keeps per-trajectory causal fp64 moments. **Config B** (ρ 0.9, λ_rel 30) is FR33's condition, the pre-declared fold1 tie-band twin of LSK's A (λ 10). The basis is computed from model.pth
at load.
- The moment update runs on a third KD6 side stream.
- The critical path gets the hook GEMM plus 2 apply kernels.
- It is self-tested at build, and any failure turns it off.

Code only: no new weights or fits. Built and verified by LSB (experiments/lsb/notes.md).
