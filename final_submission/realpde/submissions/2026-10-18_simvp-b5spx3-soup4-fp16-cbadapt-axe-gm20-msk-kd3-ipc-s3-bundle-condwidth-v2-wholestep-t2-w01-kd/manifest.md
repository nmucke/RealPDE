## Caveats (same as K3F-KD3-IPC)
- The platform's IPC and timer placement are NOT public; the gain exists only if the timer spans the round trip AND
  the transport is torch's multiprocessing reducer, otherwise ≈ 0.
- Residual risk: a harness receiving inputs through torch shared memory but plain-pickling OUR outputs inside the timer
  would ship the 1.5 MB storage once per view (+10…+26 ms/step in IPC's emulation). Each retained view keeps the whole
  1.5 MB storage alive. An enlarging `resize_()` on a returned view would grow into its neighbour (no evaluator does
  this).
