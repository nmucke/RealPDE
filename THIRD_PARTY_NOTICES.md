## Competition data

The competition data (RealPDE-Competition-Data, https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI4Science-WestlakeU/RealPDE-Competition-Data,
CC BY-NC 4.0) are not included in this repository. The following small files are derived from the data and fall
under the data's license (CC BY-NC 4.0, attribution to the RealPDEBench authors):

- `starting_kit/example_data/mean_std_real.pt` (organisers' normalisation statistics), in
  `final_submission/realpde/starting_kit/`. The `example_data/test_real/*.h5` files next
  to it are synthetic (generated by `make_example.py`).
- `weights/final_retrain_a100/` (`model.pth`, `prior.npz`, `ke_net_flow.pt`, `cond_width_weights.pt`): model
  weights and condition-prior statistics trained on `train_sim` and `train_real`. They are distributed under
  CC BY-NC 4.0, not under the MIT license.
- The constants and the compressed occupancy-mask templates fitted on `train_real` in
  `src/parameter_estimation.py` (in `final_submission/realpde/src/` and its copies).
